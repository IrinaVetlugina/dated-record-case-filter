# "CASE FILTER" & "MACRO-OS" ECOSYSTEM (`v1.3` — `v7.0`)
**Portable text actions (macros) for strict fact auditing, legal contract verification, multi-layer text deconstruction, and AI hallucination defense.**

> **In plain English:** A suite of ready-to-use text protocols (working like *Photoshop Actions*, but for text) requiring zero coding or plugins. Paste the chosen `.md` file at the start of a chat with any LLM (**ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek**) to switch the model from generating plausible-sounding noise into a strict fact auditor.

## Quick Start (30 Seconds)

1. Open a new session in any modern LLM (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek).
2. Copy the full contents of the selected `.md` file (e.g., `macro-os-v7.0-tribunal-en.md`) and paste it as your first prompt.
3. Once calibrated, run your text using the trigger commands:
   - `ULTRA: [text]` — full 10-layer diagnostic card + clean rewrite.
   - `ONION: [text]` — strips marketing fluff and unverified plans down to core facts `E`.
   - `PAIR-CHECK: [Text A] /// [Text B]` — exposes hidden substitutions between a sales pitch and a legal contract.
   - `TRIBUNAL: [disputed clause]` — adversarial review by Prosecutor, Defender, and Judge with a Divergence Index.
   - `MIRROR` — forces the AI to audit its own previous response for hallucinations.

## Sample Output (`PAIR-CHECK` in `v5.0+`)

**Input:**
- *Text A (Sales Pitch):* "We guarantee Top-3 search rankings in 2 months and 500 qualified leads, or your money back."
- *Text B (Contract):* "Contractor provides SEO consulting services over 60 business days. Services are deemed accepted upon delivery of an activity report; no refunds apply."

**System Verdict:**
- **Artifact Substitution (`E → [R]`):** Guaranteed rankings and leads are replaced by a paper activity report.
- **Hidden Window Shift:** 2 calendar months are silently extended to 60 business days (~3 calendar months).
- **Conclusion:** The contract voids every guarantee made in the pitch. Do not sign without replacing the subject of the agreement with Text A's KPIs.
