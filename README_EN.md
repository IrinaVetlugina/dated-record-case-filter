## Core Principle: The Dated Record Method

Every statement is validated against the fundamental event formula:

> **Ω(T) —[act σ(O)]→ E ∈ σ(O)**

- **Ω(T) (Frame):** Potential, rules, or plans laid out beforehand (*"what"*). Does not act on its own.
- **Act σ(O):** An irreversible transition paid for with concrete ticks—time, money, measurements, or signed documents—performed by an active executor (*"who"* and *"by what means"*).
- **E (Event):** A verified artifact recorded in a specific observer's ledger (`σ(O)`) within a closed verification window `[t₁, t₂]`.

**The Golden Rule:** *Almost every manipulation, bureaucratic brush-off, or AI hallucination relies on putting a passive process, abstraction, or undated wish into the executor's seat without paying the ticks of action.*

## Quick Start (3 Steps)

1. **Choose your tier:** Open `macro-os-v5.0-deep-engine-en.md` or `macro-os-v6.0-architect-en.md` and copy the entire text.
2. **Initialize the AI:** Paste the text as your very first message in a new chat session (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or DeepSeek).
3. **Run commands:**
   - `ULTRA: [paste statement]` — Run a 10-layer forensic audit + plain-English rewrite.
   - `ONION: [paste long article or PR release]` — Strip fluff and plans down to the Dry Core `E` and get a `TEXT PURITY RATIO %`.
   - `PAIR-CHECK: [Text A] /// [Text B]` — Compare a marketing promise against a contract clause to expose hidden substitutions.
   - `DEGENERATION: [paste rule or hypothesis]` — Test where a policy breaks at `0%` and `100%` extremes.
   - `CONSTRUCTOR: [describe obligation]` — Draft a zero-loophole contract clause with active subjects and strict deadlines.
   - `DEVIL'S ADVOCATE: [paste your clause]` — Simulate an opposing lawyer attacking your text and generate patches.
   - `MIRROR` — Force the AI to audit its own previous answer and calculate its `HONESTY INDEX %`.

## Example: Before & After (`PAIR-CHECK`)

- **Text A (Agency Pitch):** *"We guarantee TOP-3 search rankings in 2 months and 500 leads, or your money back."*
- **Text B (Actual Contract):** *"Contractor provides informational SEO consulting for 60 business days. Services are deemed rendered upon delivery of a work report; no refunds apply to rendered services."*
- **MACRO-OS Verdict:** `ARTIFACT SUBSTITUTION (E → [R]) + LIABILITY REMOVAL`. Text B replaces measurable KPIs (rankings/leads) with a paper report, extends 2 calendar months to 60 business days (~3 months), and voids the refund guarantee.
